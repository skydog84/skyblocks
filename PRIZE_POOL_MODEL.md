# SkyBlocks — The Million-Player Prize Pool
### Official model · SkyDog · July 17, 2026

## The Big Idea (owner's vision, on the record)

**Nothing launches until SkyBlocks hits 1,000,000 users.** That's the switch.
The moment the player count crosses one million, the **$1,000,000 monthly prize pool**
turns on — and it's announced from day one, so the countdown itself is the marketing:

> **"When SkyBlocks hits 1,000,000 players, the $1,000,000 monthly prize pool unlocks.
> Bring your friends. Unlock the million."**

Every player becomes a recruiter, because every player wants the pool to switch on.
The prize costs $0 until the audience that funds it actually exists.

## The Ladder (the 10× rule)

Dead simple to say, impossible to forget: **each place down wins a tenth.**

| Place | Monthly prize |
|---|---|
| 1st | $1,000,000 |
| 2nd | $100,000 |
| 3rd | $10,000 |
| 4th | $1,000 |
| 5th | $100 |
| 6th | $10 |
| **Total** | **$1,111,110 / month** |

Recommended add-on so the middle of the pack stays hungry: **top 1,000 players get
their next month free**, and small **weekly mini-pots** so regular players taste
winning between the big monthly payouts.

## How It's Funded

- Subscription: ~$3.99/month per player.
- **The pool = ~28% of gross revenue** (at 1M subscribers: $4M gross → $1.11M pool).
- The percentage scales at every size, so the model never pays out money that
  doesn't exist.

**⚠️ The one distinction that matters:** 1,000,000 *users* is not 1,000,000
*subscribers*. The $1M pool is funded by subscription revenue, so the honest trigger
is either (a) 1M paying subscribers, or (b) "pool = 28% of whatever revenue exists,
headline unlocks at 1M subs." Decide with the lawyer which phrasing goes public —
the countdown marketing works either way.

## How Winners Are Ranked

**Best verified score of the month** — not raw hours played. Reasons:
1. Score = skill. Skill-based contests are the legally safer lane.
2. "Most playtime" is trivially cheated (phone left running overnight).
3. A score race makes better content — leaderboard screenshots, "beat this" clips.

Playtime can still count as tiebreaker or bonus-entry fuel.

## What Must Be True Before the Switch Flips (the honest checklist)

1. **Gaming/sweepstakes attorney signs off.** Pay-to-enter + cash prizes is regulated;
   skill-based real-money tournaments are legal in most (not all) US states. Needs
   official rules, geo-restrictions, 18+, identity verification on payouts. Non-negotiable.
2. **Server-side scores + anti-cheat.** With $1M on the line, the game can no longer
   trust the player's phone. Accounts, verified score submission, replay checks.
3. **Payment + payout rails.** Subscriptions through the store; prize payouts with
   tax reporting (1099s in the US).
4. **A reserve.** Hold 2–3 months of prize pool in the bank before the first one is
   ever announced as live.

## The Road There (phases)

- **Phase 1 — NOW:** Free game + ads. Grow. The challenge-a-friend loop, daily
  streaks, and sound are live as of tonight — the sharing machine is running.
- **Phase 2 — First leaderboard money:** Free-entry monthly prize ($500–1,000 from
  ad revenue, "no purchase necessary"). Builds the leaderboard tech + the habit of
  SkyBlocks paying winners, with far lighter legal weight.
- **Phase 3 — THE SWITCH:** 1M users reached → subscriptions on → $1M monthly pool
  live, announced everywhere. The countdown that got us here becomes the headline
  that keeps us growing.

*This document is the working model, not legal advice. Phase 3 launches only after
counsel review.*
