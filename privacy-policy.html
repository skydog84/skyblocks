SkyBlocks Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SkyBlocks mobile game (“SkyBlocks,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you play. SkyBlocks is a single-player block-puzzle game. We designed it to be simple: you do not create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email, or any personal details to play.

The short version: SkyBlocks does not collect personal information directly. It shows ads through Google AdMob, and those ads may use a device identifier to work. You can turn ads off with a one-time in-app purchase. That's it.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any account login. Your game progress and high scores are stored only on your own device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

SkyBlocks displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use certain information automatically, such as a mobile advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location inferred from your IP address, and basic device and usage data. This is used to show ads and, where permitted, to make them more relevant.

On Apple devices, iOS asks for your permission before any app can track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads may still appear but will be less personalized.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn more about how Google uses information from partner apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-app purchases

SkyBlocks offers a one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your card number or billing details. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

4. Children's privacy

SkyBlocks is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

5. Data security

Because SkyBlocks does not collect or store personal information on our own servers, there is no personal account data for us to lose. Any advertising data is handled by Google under its own security practices.

6. Your choices

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising). You can remove ads entirely through the in-app “Remove Ads” purchase. You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at skydog8426@gmail.com.
