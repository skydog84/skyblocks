Common questions

How do I remove ads?
Tap "Remove Ads — $2.99" on the start screen. It's a one-time purchase that removes all ads forever.

I bought Remove Ads on another device / reinstalled — how do I get it back?
Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID. Reinstall the app and it will restore automatically, or email us and we'll help.

How is my score saved?
Your best score is stored right on your device — no account needed.
